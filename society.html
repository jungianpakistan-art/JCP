SOCIETY

What is happening?

A professional community for Analytical Psychology is developing in Pakistan.

What is a Developing Group?

A Developing Group is an IAAP-supported structure for developing Analytical Psychology in a country or region where the field is not yet established.

What does it do?

It brings together people with a serious professional interest in Analytical Psychology and supports study, exchange and the development of the field within its local cultural context.

Can I be involved?

Psychotherapists and psychiatrists interested in this work can express their interest below.

EXPRESS INTEREST

Expressing interest does not constitute membership or admission to a Developing Group.
