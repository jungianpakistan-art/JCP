BEGIN ANALYSIS

Jungian Analysis takes place at least once a week, at a regular agreed time. More frequent sessions may sometimes be appropriate. Less frequent sessions can be accommodated for limited periods when circumstances require it, but regularity and continuity are important to the work.

Sessions are held online. Fees are paid monthly in advance. At least 24 hours’ notice is required for cancellation or rescheduling. Sessions missed without sufficient notice are charged in full.

Up to four sessions may be missed for travel or other planned absences during the year.

HOW TO BEGIN

The first step is to complete a short form telling us something about you and what brings you to analysis.

When a place becomes available, you may be contacted for a free 20–30 minute online exploratory meeting. This gives us an opportunity to meet, talk about what brings you to analysis, and consider whether working together would be appropriate.

There is no commitment to begin analysis at this stage. If another form of psychological work seems more appropriate, this may also be discussed.
