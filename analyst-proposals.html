FOR ANALYSTS

Jung Center Pakistan welcomes conversations with Jungian analysts interested in contributing to the development of Analytical Psychology in Pakistan.

There are currently two ways to work with JCP:

OFFER A SEMINAR OR WORKSHOP

JCP welcomes proposals from Jungian analysts interested in offering online seminars, lectures, workshops or conversations to audiences in Pakistan.

Analysts are invited to tell us what they would like to offer. Proposals are considered individually, and details are discussed before an event is confirmed.

MAKE YOUR DIGITAL WORK AVAILABLE THROUGH JCP

JCP is beginning conversations with Jungian analysts who have existing digital work, including recorded lectures and seminars, publications, audio and other downloadable material.

Selected work may later be made available to audiences in Pakistan through JCP. Territorial pricing and any other arrangements are discussed with the analyst before anything is made available.

SUBMISSION TERMS

Submission of a proposal does not constitute acceptance or create an agreement with JCP.

JCP retains discretion over the work, events and collaborations it accepts, hosts, lists, promotes or continues to offer.

Live seminars, lectures and workshops are considered individually. Details are agreed separately before an event is confirmed.

For digital work, the analyst confirms that they own or control the rights necessary to authorize its sale and distribution through JCP.

Where territorial pricing is agreed, JCP will take reasonable measures to maintain the intended territorial restriction.

Submission of digital material does not authorize JCP to sell, publish or distribute it. Any work and arrangements must first be agreed by both parties.
